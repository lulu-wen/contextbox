This page runs on your own computer (http://127.0.0.1). What you type only goes into ~/.contextbox/data.db — it is not sent anywhere. Every field saves itself when you click away, and what gets saved is the normalized value (ROC years become Gregorian, phone numbers become +886), so after a save you will see the box rewritten into the standard form. Leaving things blank is fine; you can come back and fill them in any time.
